Our story

It started in a college hostel. Our squad wanted to enter a local Free Fire MAX tournament, but slots were handled in a WhatsApp group, rosters lived in screenshots and nobody knew who had actually paid or registered.

We built Booyah Arena so that captains can register once, keep their roster up to date and lock a tournament slot in a single click. Organisers see exactly who is in, and players get a profile that remembers their kills and headshot rate.

What you can do here

Captains

Create a squad, manage four players and register for events before slots run out.

Players

Keep your in-game name, role and stats in one profile that follows you between squads.

Organisers

Publish tournaments, set slots and prize pools, and watch registrations fill live.

Tournament rules

Registration

  • One account per captain. Login is required to register a squad.
  • A squad can enter a tournament only once.
  • Registration closes when all slots are filled or the event is marked Completed.

Fair play

  • No hacks, scripts or third-party tools that give an unfair advantage.
  • Teaming with other squads in a match is not allowed.
  • Organisers may remove a squad that breaks these rules.
